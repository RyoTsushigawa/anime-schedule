Other ways: a list file, or pasted titles

Or a list file (AniList or MyAnimeList)

  1. AniList: a list export from AniList's settings, or from a backup tool, as .json or .xml.
  2. MyAnimeList: Profile menu → Export (myanimelist.net/panel.php?go=export), a .xml.gz file.
  3. Choose or drop it here. Zipped .gz files are fine.

Or paste titles

One title per line. You'll need to choose benchmarks by hand, and only a list file lets you import scores back automatically.

Site median scores (optional)

Shows you never scored start their search at the community median. They load automatically when this page has them. To use newer ones, load a site-scores.json made by the helper.

Compare to others

Follow on AniList

Add people by AniList username; their list loads straight from AniList (it has to be public). Your own username keeps your list in sync: shows you complete on AniList join your queue the next time you open this page.

Or load a list file

Load one AniList or MAL list file per person, as many people as you like. Their scores then show next to yours and the site median while you rate, and in the results. Loading a list under a name that's already here replaces that person's list. Fern (fern.ignoffo.dev) can export any public AniList by username.

Export & backup

Save scores to AniList, download results, or move your progress to another browser.

How to import it
  1. AniList: set your scoring system to 10 Point Decimal (or 100 Point) in Settings → Lists, then upload under Settings → Import Lists → MyAnimeList (anilist.co/settings/import).
  2. MyAnimeList: tick the round-to-whole-numbers box first, then upload at myanimelist.net/import.php using MyAnimeList Import.
  3. The file holds every scored show and benchmark. Shows are matched by their MyAnimeList ID.
