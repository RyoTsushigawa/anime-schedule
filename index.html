A quick guide

Choose your plan

Solo shows one person’s plan. Group combines the people you select. Select nobody to show nothing.

Pick a view

Week follows the group’s watch days. Catch-up lists missed seasonal episodes. Non-seasonal keeps older shows at your own pace.

Watch, then tick ✓

Service buttons open the show. Connect an account to use ✓, Drop and scores. Ticking an episode sets your progress up to that episode, including earlier ones. Use Undo or Ctrl/⌘+Z for mistakes.

Catch up your way

Single episodes keeps each show’s episodes together in order. Mark all as watched first lets you exclude shows with their ✓; press again to save.

Connect your lists

The account icon connects AniList or MyAnimeList. With both connected, select the matching account and enable Sync both lists to mirror new changes made here. Existing lists are not copied.

Make it yours

Settings controls links, viewers, time and colour. More options contains refresh, backups and shortcuts. The planner and ranker are below the schedule.

Move a show

With editing access, use Edit schedule. Drag a show’s ⇄ onto a day or slot, then save the destination. Dropping on another show shares its time slot. Click ⇄ to enter a destination instead. Saved moves are shared with everyone.
