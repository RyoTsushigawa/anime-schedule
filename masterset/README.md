# Piplup Line Masterset

A tracker for every Piplup and Empoleon artwork plus Piplup cameos, with daily Cardmarket prices, shipping estimates to Germany and orders grouped by seller.

Progress is saved in your browser only. Use the Backup tab to move it to another device.

Card images belong to their respective owners (The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK). This is a personal, non-commercial fan project.
